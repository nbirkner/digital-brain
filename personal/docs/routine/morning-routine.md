# Morning Routine

*Replace with your actual routine.*

## Current routine (weekdays)

**6:30am** — Wake up. No phone for first 30 minutes.
**6:35am** — Water (16oz) + any morning supplements
**6:40am** — 10 minutes: sit quietly, set intention for the day
**6:50am** — Movement: 20-30 minutes (walk, workout, stretching — whatever)
**7:20am** — Shower, get ready
**7:45am** — Breakfast
**8:00am** — Review today's priorities (5 minutes, not a deep dive)
**8:15am** — Start work

## What makes this work

- No social media until after 9am
- Coffee after water, not before
- Workout clothes set out the night before

## What breaks it

- Late nights (anything after 11pm wrecks the 6:30 wake-up)
- Phone first thing in the morning
- Skipping the 10-minute sit

## Weekend

Looser. 7:30am target. Same no-phone-first-30-minutes rule.
