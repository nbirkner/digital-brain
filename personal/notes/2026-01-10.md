# January 10

Slow morning. Finally made time for the customer interview synthesis I've been putting off. Three patterns are jumping out:

1. People don't understand what "workspace" means in our product. They keep calling it a "project" even after onboarding. Language mismatch.

2. Power users want keyboard shortcuts everywhere. The mouse-heavy workflow is a real pain point for anyone doing 10+ actions per day.

3. The "team view" feature is completely invisible. Three out of three interviewees didn't know it existed even though they would use it daily if they did.

Need to bring pattern 3 to Sam — this is a discoverability problem, not a feature gap.

Lunch with Jordan to talk about the sprint. He's worried about scope creep on the onboarding redesign. Fair concern. I need to be more decisive about what stays out.

Evening: starting "Continuous Discovery Habits." Already regretting not reading it sooner.
